What NŪMEROS is

NŪMEROS works out numbers from your first names, your birth surname and your date of birth, using Pythagorean numerology, and offers texts based on those numbers. It is meant for entertainment and personal reflection.

The texts do not predict anything and are no substitute for advice from a health, legal or financial professional. Your decisions are your own. Results not guaranteed.
